## Purpose

`academy.atlashub.si` is the learning and capability layer of the AtlasHub ecosystem.

Planned areas:

- **Courses** — structured learning programs
- **Masterclasses** — focused expert sessions
- **Workshops** — applied operating labs and executive workshops
- **Learning Paths** — role and capability-based progression
- **Library** — learning resources and supporting materials
- **Members** — progress, saved resources and access rights
- **Certifications** — future assessed learning paths
- **Partners** — selected external learning and affiliate resources

## Relationship with Editions

- **AtlasHub Editions** publishes knowledge.
- **AtlasHub Academy** turns knowledge into capability.
- **AtlasHub Solutions** turns capability into implementation.

## Development workflow

1. Develop and validate in `nexflowx-hub/Academy.AtlasHub.Si`.
2. Promote approved releases to `atlashub-digital/Academy.AtlasHub.Si`.
